Email Bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Email Bot is an internal tool used only by [Company name] staff to manage customer support inboxes for our own brands. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: messages, labels and metadata in our own company Gmail accounts, used to sort, label and reply to customer emails.
  • Slack: our company Slack workspace, used to post internal notifications.

How data is used

Data is used only to handle customer support for our brands. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Email Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored on our company computer. Email content is not stored by the tool beyond what is needed to process each message.

Access and removal

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings under third-party connections.
